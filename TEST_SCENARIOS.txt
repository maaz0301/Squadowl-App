  message on APK saying trading not available (only on WEB), or a way to
  have a trade version between teams - kinda tough with small screen,

  WHAT THE CODE DOES TODAY (findings)
   - The feature is Team Builder > generate teams > "Adjust".
   - The mobile app ALREADY has it (TeamAdjuster, added Sept 8, commit
     81475c3): long-press and drag a player onto another team, OR tap a
     player and pick the destination from a sheet. Both use the same
     move logic, so undo/redo works for both.
   - So a "not available on APK" message would be wrong for a build that
     contains it. Likely reasons Shawn did not see it:
       (a) his APK was built before Sept 8 / before this branch;
       (b) the "Adjust" button is only enabled AFTER teams are
           generated, and is easy to miss;
       (c) long-press drag may not work well on his Android device
           (unconfirmed - needs a device test).

  MY RECOMMENDATION (thoughts for Shawn)
   1. Do NOT add a "web only" message. Rebuild the APK and confirm the
      adjuster is present.
   2. Keep BOTH ways: tap-to-move sheet is the reliable phone way; drag
      is a bonus. This is exactly the small-screen answer he asked for.
   3. Make it easier to find: hint text under the Adjust button
      ("Long-press and drag a player, or tap one to move them") and a
      clear "Move" affordance on each player row.
   4. Only if long-press drag proves unreliable on Android: keep the
      sheet as the documented method, and show a short hint instead of
      hiding the feature.
